The Candidate Built by Adversity.
Forged in Action.

Rene Campos is a Fresno-born constitutional and civil rights advocate — shaped not by privilege but by lived experience, survival, and an unshakable belief in equal justice for every American.

Raised by his mother Diana, he learned early that leadership is earned through service, not titles. Before any platform, he was already doing the work — quietly supporting individuals with disabilities, navigating bureaucracy so they wouldn't lose the support they depend on to live.

He has survived homelessness. He stepped into the role of family protector after losing his mother to COVID. He rose to corporate leadership — then filed formal complaints when others looked away. He does not speak about systems from the outside. He has tested them.
